Save a PDF

Keep a digital copy or take it to a printer. Export at 150 or 300 dpi, with optional bleed. Printing is purchased separately.

Books supports up to 200 pages. PDFs are RGB and include a front cover; check your printer’s size, binding, back-cover, and spine requirements before ordering. Printer-specific covers and physical proofing are not included.

Before downloading

  • SystemRequires an Apple silicon Mac, M1 or newer, running macOS 14 Sonoma or later.
  • Free editionNo licensing limit on library size. Books, PDF export, and cleanup are included. Disk space and indexing time still apply.
  • Media sourcesWorks with readable folders, external drives, and mounted network shares. Cloud-only placeholders must be downloaded first.
  • FormatsImages: JPG, JPEG, PNG, HEIC, HEIF, WebP, GIF, TIFF, and TIF. Videos: MOV, MP4, and M4V.
  • Photos.appAnthora does not read a Photos.app library directly.
  • First launchThis direct download is not notarized by Apple. The install guide explains the one-time macOS Open confirmation. Gatekeeper does not need to be disabled.
  • Analysis limitsAutomatic matches and quality signals require review. Semantic language search, best-shot grouping, and crop-aware matching are not included in this release.
